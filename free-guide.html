Inside the guide

Simple ideas for building better routines.

The guide is designed as a practical starting point for pet families and can be adapted to different animals and households.

Everyday routines

Think through feeding, rest, activity, grooming and observation as parts of a consistent daily routine.

Feeding basics

Consider species, life stage, appropriate food and a routine that fits your pet's individual needs.

Enrichment ideas

Add safe opportunities for movement, exploration, play and natural behaviors.

Responsible care

Learn your pet's normal patterns and seek qualified professional advice when something seems unusual or concerning.
