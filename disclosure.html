Product disclosure

How product information is presented.

A straightforward explanation of how product-related information may appear on Paws & Cuddles.

Important: This is a starter disclosure template. Review and customize it to match the actual products, links, partnerships and business arrangements used by the website.

1. Product information

Paws & Cuddles may discuss or display pet products as part of educational content, product guides or general shopping resources.

Product information is provided for convenience and general educational purposes. It does not mean that a particular product is appropriate for every animal or situation.

2. Product suitability

Before purchasing or using a pet product, consider your companion's species, size, age, behavior, environment and individual needs.

Always check current manufacturer instructions, warnings, specifications and safety information before using a product.

3. Pricing and availability

Product prices, specifications, availability and promotions can change without notice.

When a product is linked to a retailer or manufacturer, check the destination website for the most current information before making a purchase.

4. Affiliate relationships

If Paws & Cuddles uses affiliate links in the future, those links should be clearly identified as affiliate links or otherwise disclosed in a way that is appropriate for the relevant audience and applicable requirements.

An affiliate relationship may mean that Paws & Cuddles receives a qualifying commission if you make a purchase through a designated link, without necessarily changing the price you pay.

5. Editorial independence

Product information should be presented based on the purpose of the content and relevant product characteristics rather than creating misleading impressions about a product's suitability or performance.

6. Third-party retailers and manufacturers

Purchases made through third-party retailers or manufacturers are subject to their own terms, policies, shipping arrangements, warranties, returns and customer-service procedures.

Paws & Cuddles is not responsible for changes made by those third parties to their products, websites, pricing or policies.

7. Safety

Always follow product instructions and supervise pets when appropriate. Do not use a damaged product or an item that appears unsuitable for your animal.

For products related to health, medication or specialized care, seek advice from an appropriately qualified professional.

8. Transparency

If the website develops commercial relationships, sponsored content or affiliate partnerships, this disclosure should be updated so that those relationships are described clearly and accurately.
