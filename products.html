Helpful categories for pet families

🧼 Grooming

Consider brushes, grooming tools and cleaning products based on the animal's coat, skin, size and individual needs.

🥣 Feeding

Feeding bowls, storage containers and related accessories should be practical, suitable and easy to keep clean.

🧸 Toys & Enrichment

Choose enrichment items that are appropriate for the species, size and abilities of the animal using them.

🏠 Everyday Essentials

Everyday accessories can help create comfortable, practical spaces for pets while supporting their normal routines.

Before you buy

Things worth considering

🐾 Suitability

Check whether the item is appropriate for your pet's species, size, age and individual needs.

📏 Size & Fit

Make sure products such as beds, carriers, collars, harnesses and habitats are appropriately sized.

🧽 Cleaning

Consider how easily an item can be cleaned and maintained as part of your regular routine.

🛡️ Safe Use

Follow the manufacturer's instructions and inspect products regularly for damage or wear.

🌿 Materials

Consider the materials used and whether they are suitable for the intended animal and environment.

📋 Instructions

Read product instructions carefully before using equipment, accessories or specialized items.

Safety reminder: Products should be used according to their instructions and supervised when appropriate. If an item becomes damaged or unsafe, stop using it and replace it as needed.
