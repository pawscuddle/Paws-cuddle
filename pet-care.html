Pet care

Practical foundations for everyday pet life.

Explore simple starting points for caring for different kinds of companion animals while keeping their individual needs in mind.

Explore by pet

Care ideas for different companions

🐶

Dogs

Build routines around balanced feeding, exercise, enrichment, grooming, rest and positive interaction.

🐱

Cats

Think about nutrition, litter areas, scratching, play, grooming, quiet spaces and opportunities to explore.

🐰

Rabbits

Provide appropriate food, clean living areas, movement, enrichment and safe opportunities to explore.

🐦

Birds

Consider species-appropriate nutrition, clean housing, enrichment, safe exercise and social interaction.

🐹

Small Pets

Small companions still need thoughtful habitats, suitable food, enrichment, cleanliness and gentle handling.

🦎

Reptiles & Aquatic Pets

Their environments can be highly specialized, so focus on species-specific habitat, temperature, water quality and feeding requirements.

Everyday routines

Small routines can make a big difference

🥣 Feeding

Choose food appropriate for the species and follow suitable feeding routines.

🧼 Grooming

Keep grooming needs in mind, including coat, nails, feathers or other species-specific care.

🏃 Exercise

Provide safe movement and activity that suits the animal's species, age and individual needs.

🧸 Enrichment

Offer safe opportunities for exploration, play, natural behaviors and mental engagement.

A helpful reminder: Pet-care information is general education. Animals can have very different needs depending on species, age, environment and individual circumstances. For health concerns or unusual changes in behavior, consult an appropriately qualified veterinary professional.
