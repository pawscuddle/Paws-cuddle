Pet care library

Practical foundations for everyday pet life.

Use these starting points to organize routines around your companion's species, environment and individual needs.

By pet type

Start with the needs that matter most.

Every animal is different. These general guides are designed to help pet families think about everyday routines, enrichment, comfort and responsible care.

Dogs

Build predictable routines around appropriate movement, positive interactions, rest, grooming and everyday observation.

Needs can vary by age, breed, size, health and individual dog.

Cats

Support natural behaviors with safe spaces, play, scratching opportunities, comfortable resting areas and calm daily rhythms.

Individual personality, age and environment can affect what works best.

Rabbits

Think about suitable housing, gentle handling, appropriate food, clean living areas and opportunities for natural behaviors.

Rabbit care can be quite specialized, so species-appropriate guidance is important.

Birds

Consider species-appropriate housing, social needs, enrichment, safe surroundings and consistent daily routines.

Bird needs differ substantially between species and individuals.

Small Pets

Provide suitable habitats, appropriate bedding, balanced food, careful handling and enrichment suited to the animal.

Small pets can have very different housing and dietary requirements.

Reptiles

Pay close attention to species-specific habitat requirements, temperature, lighting, diet and safe handling.

Reptile care is highly species-specific and should be researched carefully.

Fish & Aquatic Pets

Focus on water quality, suitable environments, compatible inhabitants, feeding routines and gradual environmental changes.

Aquarium requirements vary considerably by species and setup.

Everyday routines

Small habits can support a comfortable routine.

Explore a few practical areas that can become part of your everyday pet-care routine.

Feeding

Keep meals consistent and choose food appropriate for the animal's species, life stage and individual needs.

Grooming

Regular grooming can help maintain comfort and cleanliness while giving you opportunities to notice changes.

Exercise

Choose movement and activity that are suitable for the species, age, ability and environment of your pet.

Toys & Enrichment

Enrichment can encourage natural behaviors and add variety to everyday life when it is safe and appropriate.

A useful starting point

Pay attention to the individual animal.

General pet-care information can be helpful, but no two animals are exactly alike. Age, species, breed, environment, behavior and existing needs can all affect an appropriate routine.

When something seems unusual or you have concerns about your pet's health or wellbeing, seek advice from a qualified veterinarian or appropriate animal-care professional.

Keep learning

Good pet care is an ongoing process. Learn about your companion's normal routines, provide a suitable environment and make changes thoughtfully rather than all at once.
